Emergency kit

Get help without internet

Calls and ordinary SMS use mobile signal, not internet. With no mobile signal either, this app cannot send to nearby phones or discover people. Use the sound and visible signal below to attract someone within hearing or sight.

Call emergency dispatch · 112

India: medical, police and fire assistance. Elsewhere use your local emergency number. Emergency calls may work on another available network, but are not guaranteed.

Send your location by SMS

No location added.

GPS can work without internet, but acquisition may take longer. Your phone opens an SMS draft; you must send it. Sending and delivery are not confirmed by B fox.

Reach nearby people

Sound is off.

Raise your volume if safe. A phone speaker has limited range; this is not an alert sent to nearby devices. Keep the screen open for sound. If there is no response, use a whistle or shout in bursts. Do not move into floodwater, smoke or unstable buildings to find help.

Immediate safety

Flood

Move to higher ground. Never enter moving floodwater.

Earthquake

Drop, cover and hold on. Move outside only after shaking stops.

Fire or smoke

Stay low, use stairs, not lifts. Leave by a safe exit.

Based on NDMA India preparedness guidance. Location is shared only when you send your SMS. Saved contacts remain on this device until removed; anyone using the device can view them.
